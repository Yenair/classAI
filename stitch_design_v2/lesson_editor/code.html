Introduction

Introduction to Optics and Light Waves

Optics is the branch of physics that studies the behaviour and properties of light, including its interactions with matter and the construction of instruments that use or detect it. In this lesson, we will explore the fundamental principles of reflection, refraction, and the dual nature of light as both a wave and a particle.

MCQ • Cognitive: Identify

1. Which phenomenon best illustrates the wave nature of light?

A) Photoelectric effect
B) Diffraction
C) Rectilinear propagation
D) Blackbody radiation
Theory • Cognitive: Calculate, Analyze

2. A light ray travels from air into a glass block with a refractive index of 1.52.

If the angle of incidence is 45°, calculate the angle of refraction. State Snell's Law and explain how the speed of light changes as it enters the glass block.

Suggested Rubric / Key

  • Calculation of angle (approx 28°): 3 marks
  • Correct statement of Snell's Law: 2 marks
  • Explanation of speed reduction in denser medium: 2 marks
