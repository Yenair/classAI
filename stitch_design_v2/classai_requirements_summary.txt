# ClassAI Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## App Overview
Offline-first AI teaching assistant for Nigerian schools. Flutter/Material 3.

## Target Users
Nigerian teachers and admins on low-end Android devices (2G/3G).

## Key Screens
1. **Login / Activation**: Email/password, role-based recovery (OTP/Admin instructions).
2. **Dashboard (Editor - Tablet)**: NavRail, Sync AppBar, Conflict Banner, Recent Lessons.
3. **Dashboard (Reader - Phone)**: BottomNav, Compact Cards, Sync AppBar.
4. **Lesson Editor**: Subject/Class/Week/Topic inputs, Bloom's chips, Segmented question types, AI Generate, Preview cards.
5. **Exam Editor**: Similar to Lesson Editor, MCQ/Theory generation.
6. **Vault Browser**: Filtered grid of teaching resources, credit costs, acquisition flow.
7. **Vault Publisher**: Submission form for resources, status tracker.
8. **Admin Dashboard**: Tabs for Teacher Mgmt (sync status dots), Vault Approval, Sync Health.
9. **Payment Screen**: ₦5k/₦10k/₦25k plans, Paystack flow.
10. **Settings**: Mode override, Theme toggle, Term boundaries.

## Technical Constraints
- WCAG AAA Contrast (Primary #1A2B3C on White).
- Offline-first sync indicators.
- Large touch targets (≥16pt text).
- EmergencyFlushService handling for forms.
- Inter typography.
