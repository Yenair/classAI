English Studies • JSS 2

Presentation

Card 2 of 4

The teacher introduces the concept of active verbs through a short narrative. Students are asked to identify actions performed by the characters.

Key Example

"The cheetah sprints across the savanna." Ask the class: What is the subject doing?

Following the example, divide the class into groups of four. Distribute worksheets containing sentences with missing verbs. Instruct groups to collaborate and fill in the blanks with the most descriptive active verbs they can think of within a 5-minute timeframe.
